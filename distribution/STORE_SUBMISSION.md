## Long description
Image Tools is a mobile image workspace for resizing, compression, format conversion, cropping, filters, retouching, creative effects and export.

Core tools include:
- AI Background Cutout with local fallback
- Resize and smart crop
- Compress and convert
- Adjust brightness, contrast and saturation
- Auto Enhance and Sharpen
- Collage and Photo Strip
- Social Canvas
- Watermark and Meme
- Blur, Vignette, Film Grain and Posterize
- Edge Detect and Color Tint
- Exposure, Gamma and RGB Balance
- Highlights & Shadows
- Rounded Corners and DuoTone

Images are processed on the device by the current editing engine. The app does not use an advertising SDK.
