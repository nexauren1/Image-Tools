# Image Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Image Tools is developed by Nexauren.

## Information we process

### Account information
When you create or use an account, authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method, this can include your email address, display name and authentication identifiers.

### Premium subscription
If you use the Premium subscription flow, the app sends an authenticated request to the Nexauren payment backend to create, check or cancel a subscription. Payment processing is handled by the payment provider. The app does not directly collect payment card details.

### Images
The current image editing tools process selected images on the device. The core editor does not upload selected photos to an image-processing server.

### App settings
The app stores local preferences such as language, dark mode and the selected output folder on the device.

## Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase services for account authentication and premium entitlement state.

The AI Background Cutout uses Google's ML Kit Subject Segmentation API. Its model is delivered through Google Play services and may be downloaded dynamically when needed.

## Data sharing
Image Tools does not sell personal information. Photos selected for editing are not intentionally uploaded by the editor to Nexauren servers.

## Security
Authentication tokens and account access are handled through Firebase Authentication. Premium entitlement is read from the authenticated user's backend state.

## Children's privacy
Image Tools is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should only create accounts where permitted by their local laws and the rules of the relevant account provider.

## Contact

## Changes
This policy may be updated when app features or third-party services change. The latest version should be published together with the current app release.
