# Image Tools Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**  
**App:** Image Tools for Android — com.nexauren.imagetools

This policy describes the current Android release and supporting website/API.

## Images and files

Normal editing tasks process selected images on the Android device. This includes resizing, compression, format conversion, cropping, rotation, filters, color adjustments, watermarking, blur/sharpen/pixelate effects, borders, palettes, collages, grid splitting, OCR, EXIF inspection, face-blur processing, GIF creation and PDF export/merging. The app does not send image contents to the Image Tools backend merely to perform these operations. The user chooses where exported files are saved. If a result is shared with another app, that destination's privacy practices apply.

## Information stored on the device

The app may save theme and output-folder preferences, favorites, recipes, processing statistics and recent-operation history locally. This information may be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Exported files saved outside the app are not deleted when the app is uninstalled.

## Account and sign-in information

When users sign in or create an account, Firebase Authentication processes information needed for that feature. Depending on the sign-in method, this can include email address, display name, Google profile information, Firebase user identifier and authentication/security event information.

## Analytics and diagnostics

The Android app includes Firebase Analytics. Depending on SDK configuration and device settings, Firebase may process app-instance identifiers, device/app technical information and usage or interaction events to measure usage and help improve reliability. Firebase's privacy terms and the app's configuration govern that provider's processing.

## Premium and payment information

If Premium is used, the app sends authenticated requests to the Cloudflare Worker to start or refresh subscription access. The backend may process a Firebase user identifier, authentication token, PayPal subscription identifier, status and transaction references. PayPal processes payment credentials, billing details and the payment/subscription transaction under its own policies. The PayPal client secret is not embedded in the APK.

## Service providers and hosting

Firebase provides authentication, Firestore entitlement storage and analytics. Cloudflare hosts the public legal pages and subscription API. PayPal handles payments. Providers may process technical request information such as IP address, timestamps and security logs. We do not sell personal information. Provider-specific policies apply to information processed by those providers.

## Retention and deletion

Local settings and history remain on the device until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Account and subscription records may be retained while needed to provide the service, reconcile payments, prevent fraud, resolve disputes or comply with law.

To request deletion of an Image Tools account and associated server-side account data, contact **[nexaurenstore@gmail.com](mailto:nexaurenstore@gmail.com?subject=Image%20Tools%20Account%20Deletion)** privately. Include the email address associated with the account and clearly state that you request deletion. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or payment credentials. Records required for legal or billing purposes may be retained as required by law.

## Children's privacy and international processing

Image Tools is not intended to knowingly collect information from children below the minimum age required by applicable law. Service providers may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence, subject to their terms and safeguards.
